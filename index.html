How it works

  1. Extract: an LLM turns the receipts, bank statement and card statement into structured data.
  2. Reconcile: an LLM matches amounts across documents and proposes one decision for each.
  3. Decide: the student makes the final call on the three hardest items.
  4. Report: plain Python, with no LLM, adds up the totals and builds the page.
